	permit ip 192.168.2.0 0.0.0.255 192.168.1.0 0.0.0.255

crypto isakmp policy 10
 encryption aes
 hash sha
 authentication pre-share
 group 2
 lifetime 86400

crypto isakmp key swiftmartcore address 92.1.0.2

crypto ipsec transform-set SwiftMartVPN-SET esp-aes esp-sha-hmac

crypto map SwiftMartVPN-MAP 10 ipsec-isakmp
 set peer 92.1.0.2
 set transform-set SwiftMartVPN-SET
 match address SwiftMartVPN


int fa1/1
